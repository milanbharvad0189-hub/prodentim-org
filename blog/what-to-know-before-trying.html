Before ordering any Oral Probiotic / Dental Health Supplement, it helps to know exactly what you are buying, how it is meant to be used, and what happens if it does not work out. Here is a straight, honest rundown of ProDentim covering each of those points.

What ProDentim Actually Contains

ProDentim lists 8 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Lactobacillus Paracasei — A lactic-acid bacterium commonly used in oral-care probiotic formulas; included to help populate the mouth with beneficial flora that compete with less desirable bacteria.
  • Lactobacillus Reuteri — A well-studied probiotic species frequently found in dental and oral probiotic blends, included to support a favorable balance of bacteria around the gums and teeth.
  • Bifidobacterium Lactis BL-04 — A documented Bifidobacterium strain used in chewable oral supplements; included as part of the formula's live-culture component for microbiome balance.
  • Inulin — A plant-derived soluble fiber that acts as food for the probiotic strains, helping them establish and thrive rather than simply passing through.
  • Malic Acid (from Strawberry) — A naturally occurring acid sourced from strawberry, included for its role in supporting a clean-feeling mouth environment and fresher breath.
  • Tricalcium Phosphate — A calcium-phosphate mineral included to support the mineral content of teeth and to help maintain a neutral, comfortable oral environment.
  • Peppermint — Provides the fresh mint taste of the chewable tablet while contributing a clean, cooling mouthfeel after each use.
  • Spearmint — A milder mint used alongside peppermint to round out flavor and leave breath feeling refreshed.

How It Is Meant To Be Used

The label calls for 1 chewable tablet per day, with 30 chewable tablets in each bottle. Some ingredient amounts are listed as part of a combined blend rather than individually. Consistency day to day matters more than any single dose.

How It Is Meant To Work

ProDentim takes the direct route: chewing releases live Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains into the mouth itself, where inulin feeds them and mint botanicals keep things feeling fresh.

Live cultures reach the mouth directly: Because the tablet is chewed and held in the mouth, the Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains are delivered exactly where they're needed rather than travelling through the digestive tract first.

Prebiotic fuel helps them stick around: Inulin gives those strains a food source in the oral environment, supporting their ability to colonize and multiply instead of being washed away.

Crowding out the unwanted bacteria: A well-populated community of beneficial bacteria leaves less room for the strains associated with plaque, gum irritation and odor.

Mineral and botanical backup: Tricalcium phosphate supports the mineral side of tooth care, while malic acid and the mint botanicals keep the mouth feeling clean and breath fresh.
