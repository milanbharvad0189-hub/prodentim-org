ProDentim is a chewable dietary supplement built around the idea that your mouth hosts its own community of bacteria. Rather than scrubbing, rinsing or masking, each tablet delivers a blend of probiotic strains plus selected plant and mineral ingredients intended to support the balance of that oral microbiome — and by extension, the comfort of your teeth and gums. It is a daily wellness habit, taken as a soft chewable tablet, meant to sit alongside normal brushing and flossing rather than replace them.

Who Makes ProDentim, And What It Is Not

ProDentim is not a toothpaste, mouthwash or a substitute for dental visits — it's a daily chewable probiotic supplement meant to work alongside the oral care you already do. That distinction matters more than marketing copy — it shapes what a reasonable buyer should expect from a Oral Probiotic / Dental Health Supplement like this one.

What ProDentim Claims To Support

  • Supports a balanced oral microbiome — delivers live probiotic strains that help keep the mix of bacteria in your mouth tipped toward the beneficial side.
  • Promotes healthy-looking teeth and gums — the combination of probiotic cultures and tricalcium phosphate is aimed at keeping teeth and gum tissue feeling and looking well cared for.
  • Helps freshen breath at the source — mint botanicals plus a better-balanced mouth environment target odor-causing bacteria rather than just masking them.
  • Complements your brushing routine — a chewable tablet slots in easily alongside brushing and flossing to add microbiome support your toothpaste can't provide.
  • Supports a clean, comfortable mouth feel — malic acid and calcium phosphate work to maintain a neutral oral environment so your mouth feels fresh through the day.
  • Feeds the good bacteria you want to keep — inulin acts as a prebiotic, giving the probiotic strains a food source so they can settle in and do their job.

Ingredients At A Glance
