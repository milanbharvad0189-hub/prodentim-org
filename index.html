The Mechanism

How Does ProDentim Work?

1

Live cultures reach the mouth directly

Because the tablet is chewed and held in the mouth, the Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains are delivered exactly where they're needed rather than travelling through the digestive tract first.

2

Prebiotic fuel helps them stick around

Inulin gives those strains a food source in the oral environment, supporting their ability to colonize and multiply instead of being washed away.

3

Crowding out the unwanted bacteria

A well-populated community of beneficial bacteria leaves less room for the strains associated with plaque, gum irritation and odor.

4

Mineral and botanical backup

Tricalcium phosphate supports the mineral side of tooth care, while malic acid and the mint botanicals keep the mouth feeling clean and breath fresh.

What's In Every Tablet

What Are The Ingredients In ProDentim?

🦠

Lactobacillus Paracasei

A lactic-acid bacterium commonly used in oral-care probiotic formulas; included to help populate the mouth with beneficial flora that compete with less desirable bacteria.

🦠

Lactobacillus Reuteri

A well-studied probiotic species frequently found in dental and oral probiotic blends, included to support a favorable balance of bacteria around the gums and teeth.

🦠

Bifidobacterium Lactis BL-04

A documented Bifidobacterium strain used in chewable oral supplements; included as part of the formula's live-culture component for microbiome balance.

🌾

Inulin

A plant-derived soluble fiber that acts as food for the probiotic strains, helping them establish and thrive rather than simply passing through.

🌿

Malic Acid (from Strawberry)

A naturally occurring acid sourced from strawberry, included for its role in supporting a clean-feeling mouth environment and fresher breath.

🧂

Tricalcium Phosphate

A calcium-phosphate mineral included to support the mineral content of teeth and to help maintain a neutral, comfortable oral environment.

🌿

Peppermint

Provides the fresh mint taste of the chewable tablet while contributing a clean, cooling mouthfeel after each use.

🌿

Spearmint

A milder mint used alongside peppermint to round out flavor and leave breath feeling refreshed.

Before You Ask Us Directly

ProDentim FAQ, Answered Honestly

It's a chewable oral probiotic supplement — a soft tablet you chew once a day that combines live probiotic strains with prebiotic fiber, a calcium mineral and mint botanicals, all aimed at supporting the balance of bacteria in your mouth.

The formula centers on Lactobacillus Paracasei, Lactobacillus Reuteri and Bifidobacterium Lactis BL-04, supported by inulin, malic acid from strawberry, tricalcium phosphate, peppermint and spearmint.

Chew one tablet daily. Slow, thorough chewing is what lets the probiotic cultures and botanicals spread through the mouth, so don't just swallow it whole.

Probiotic approaches tend to be gradual. Many users describe changes in mouth feel and breath over several weeks of consistent daily use rather than overnight, so give it a fair stretch of daily use before judging.

The ingredients are food-adjacent and generally well tolerated. Some people notice mild temporary digestive or mouth adjustment in the first days. If you're pregnant, nursing, on medication, immunocompromised or managing a health condition, check with your doctor first.
