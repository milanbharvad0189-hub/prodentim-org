How Does ProDentim Work?

1

Live cultures reach the mouth directly

Because the tablet is chewed and held in the mouth, the Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains are delivered exactly where they're needed rather than travelling through the digestive tract first.

2

Prebiotic fuel helps them stick around

Inulin gives those strains a food source in the oral environment, supporting their ability to colonize and multiply instead of being washed away.

3

Crowding out the unwanted bacteria

A well-populated community of beneficial bacteria leaves less room for the strains associated with plaque, gum irritation and odor.

4

Mineral and botanical backup

Tricalcium phosphate supports the mineral side of tooth care, while malic acid and the mint botanicals keep the mouth feeling clean and breath fresh.
