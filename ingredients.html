What Are The Ingredients In ProDentim?

🦠

Lactobacillus Paracasei

A lactic-acid bacterium commonly used in oral-care probiotic formulas; included to help populate the mouth with beneficial flora that compete with less desirable bacteria.

🦠

Lactobacillus Reuteri

A well-studied probiotic species frequently found in dental and oral probiotic blends, included to support a favorable balance of bacteria around the gums and teeth.

🦠

Bifidobacterium Lactis BL-04

A documented Bifidobacterium strain used in chewable oral supplements; included as part of the formula's live-culture component for microbiome balance.

🌾

Inulin

A plant-derived soluble fiber that acts as food for the probiotic strains, helping them establish and thrive rather than simply passing through.

🌿

Malic Acid (from Strawberry)

A naturally occurring acid sourced from strawberry, included for its role in supporting a clean-feeling mouth environment and fresher breath.

🧂

Tricalcium Phosphate

A calcium-phosphate mineral included to support the mineral content of teeth and to help maintain a neutral, comfortable oral environment.

🌿

Peppermint

Provides the fresh mint taste of the chewable tablet while contributing a clean, cooling mouthfeel after each use.

🌿

Spearmint

A milder mint used alongside peppermint to round out flavor and leave breath feeling refreshed.
