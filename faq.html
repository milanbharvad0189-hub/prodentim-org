ProDentim FAQ, Answered Honestly

It's a chewable oral probiotic supplement — a soft tablet you chew once a day that combines live probiotic strains with prebiotic fiber, a calcium mineral and mint botanicals, all aimed at supporting the balance of bacteria in your mouth.

The formula centers on Lactobacillus Paracasei, Lactobacillus Reuteri and Bifidobacterium Lactis BL-04, supported by inulin, malic acid from strawberry, tricalcium phosphate, peppermint and spearmint.

Chew one tablet daily. Slow, thorough chewing is what lets the probiotic cultures and botanicals spread through the mouth, so don't just swallow it whole.

Probiotic approaches tend to be gradual. Many users describe changes in mouth feel and breath over several weeks of consistent daily use rather than overnight, so give it a fair stretch of daily use before judging.

The ingredients are food-adjacent and generally well tolerated. Some people notice mild temporary digestive or mouth adjustment in the first days. If you're pregnant, nursing, on medication, immunocompromised or managing a health condition, check with your doctor first.
